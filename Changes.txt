# RimExodus: Delta-V changes

## Unreleased

- Undocking is no longer a separate command: thrust is the only separation trigger. Docked ships can enter pilot mode, and the first thruster output separates the moving ship from everything else on its map — a free-flying ship splits residual blocks off into independent wreck containers, while a docked ship transfers only its control domain into a new pocket container inheriting the host's point velocity and spin. Clean separations (nothing left behind) execute immediately; leftover fragments or channel contents are listed once in a single confirmation; cancelling exits pilot mode so you can simply re-enter and thrust again (the old structure-version suppression that permanently blocked thrusting after a cancel is gone). NPC ships run the same path without dialogs, docking channels retract with the departure, and the camera follows through the universe frame when your view map changes.
- Fixed the domain strip rotating with the landing rotation: docking and undocking both set the gravship rotation before stripping, and the vanilla Foundations view rotates with it — so nearly the whole hull's substructure stayed behind on the source map (things traveled but the deck didn't), spawning ghost wreck markers that replayed at the wrong map size with out-of-bounds log spam. Stripping now uses the captured absolute cells, snapshot replay sizes the regenerated map from the recorded source dimensions, and any out-of-bounds entries are skipped with one aggregated error instead of silently wrapping onto wrong cells.
- POI interiors and unmaterialized markers are now strictly separate things: a packed POI is a mapless trigger shown by its configured type; materializing it creates a plain container with no POI identity — named after the structures actually aboard (environment name as fallback for bare asteroids), drawn with per-ship silhouettes or a whole-map wreck silhouette for derelicts. Containers are archived back into markers only when no ship or pawn remains aboard and the player is beyond the delete radius (blank maps are removed outright); split-off wrecks get the same distance lifecycle instead of staying loaded forever, while maps with ships (NPC included) or pawns aboard only ever sleep, never archive. Native stations keep the vanilla mirror, materialization failures back off instead of retrying every second, and a map destroyed externally takes its container with it.
- Fixed docking site selection opening on the previous target's map: the vanilla gravship controller caches its move-designator forever and only resets it in the landing-end chain our tactical flow bypasses, so the landing marker — and the camera dragged along by its spawn callback — landed on the map of the previous docking. Site selection now builds a fresh designator bound to the current target, clears the stale vanilla cache, and can be cancelled with Esc at any time during picking.
- Docking target enumeration now excludes your own map by both container reference and map identity, and the universe enforces one-holder-per-map at registration (a duplicate holder is rejected with an error naming both). The dev universe dump identifies every map by stable unique ID with per-map ship lists, and engine-less ships can finally enter pilot mode — pocket maps have no world tile, so the old zero-gravity check misread them as gravity maps.
- "Select docking site" now enumerates every dockable object under your hull instead of silently grabbing the wrong one: your own container is excluded from the candidates (previously it always won the nearest check at distance zero, so the picker opened on your own map), and when several targets' projections overlap, a menu sorted by visible-hull distance lets you choose the target — a single candidate still goes straight to the vanilla site picker.
- Fixed the tactical docking chain after confirming the site: the confirm no longer falls through into the vanilla landing cutscene running on the hollow preview gravship (which zoomed the camera to zero and hard-froze the game), the registered landing pose no longer loses the pocket-map geometric-center term (the docking ghost, autopilot target and alignment window were all offset by about half a pocket map), and completion now runs its epilogue — focus hands over to the host map with the camera view point converted through the universe frame (zoom preserved) and the source pocket map is deleted, the docking-port channels extend and the success message fires. A stale-snapshot validity guard had silently skipped the entire epilogue, stranding the camera on the emptied pocket map.
- Fixed pilot-console and docking-port gizmo icons referencing texture paths that don't exist in 1.6 ("UI/Commands/Cancel", "UI/Commands/Claim"); they now use the vanilla Designators icons.
- Ships and SpaceObjects are now decoupled: a SpaceObject is purely a map container with orbital motion, while a ship is the structure its main pilot console claims plus the same-ownership four-connected substructure under it — one map can host several ships and unclaimed wrecks at once, and container names are the concatenation of all structure names aboard. Pilot consoles claim on spawn (a console on an ownerless domain founds a new ship and claims only its connected patch; on an already-claimed owner it defaults to backup), destroyed mains pass to a same-facing backup by stable ID, and "set as main / set as backup" gizmos swap the claim explicitly — the main console's facing defines the bow. Renaming from the main console renames the structure (the ship's name) and syncs every grav engine aboard, and renaming an engine syncs back. Reassigning ownership can no longer touch the cell under a main console.
- Tactical docking and undocking no longer require a grav engine and no longer replay the vanilla landing cutscene: when the geometric hold window completes, the ship's current control domain is captured, stripped from its pocket map and placed directly into the target map through the vanilla placement finisher — anything built during the approach travels along. Docking first confirms (with a full list) any pawns, items or fragments left outside the hull on the source map; if that list changes during the approach the docking aborts and asks again. Undocking transfers only the leaving ship's control domain into a new pocket container whose pose and velocity are inherited from the host rigid body at the hull point (spin tangent included), retracts only the docking channels related to the departure (channel contents stay behind at their cells, listed in a single confirmation together with any same-owner fragments), and never requires an engine — only one working thruster. Docked compositions now drift with their host container and passive drag instead of freezing.
- Thrusting with disconnected structures aboard now goes through a pre-thrust integrity gate: manual input, active braking and autopilot commands that would fire thrusters first present one summarized confirmation (the game pauses while it is open); confirming splits every residual block into its own independent wreck container ("Wreck #N") inheriting the current position and velocity, while cancelling aborts that thrust command without re-prompting until the structure changes. Passive drift and the drag itself never split anything, and NPC ships run the same split without a dialog.
- New passive drag setting (on by default): linear and angular velocities decay with configurable half-lives (defaults 8s / 3s), coexisting with flight assist. It is not a thruster command — no fuel, no flames, no splitting — and the online MPC autopilot predicts with the exact same drag model so braking, waypoint arrival, target tracking and station-keeping stay consistent with and without it.
- Build and landing rules on space maps: substructure may now be laid outside the grav-engine footprint (natural rock roofs still block it, surface maps keep the vanilla range rule) so new ships can be built next to existing ones — lay substructure, reassign it to a new owner, add a console to found the ship; neighboring multiple owners no longer rejects a build (the cell stays unowned for the player to divide), and a landing footprint overlapping any existing substructure is rejected — including your own leftovers — while four-adjacency to foreign substructure is now legal. Docked status is derived from what shares the map: the only ship on a map (not touching natural ground) is free-flying, several ships or a ship resting on an asteroid are docked and none of them may thrust until they undock.
- The status panel lists every ship on the focused map (name plus free-flight/docked state) and the selected row becomes the ship you view and operate; the planet globe and world markers draw per-ship silhouettes over per-container icons, shells draw once per container map, and boarding pods now target any map container (the whole map is the boarding face). Strategic gravship launches still require engines and capture only the engine field's own-ownership cells — the old whole-domain pocket-map exception is retired — and piloting state no longer persists across saves: loading a save leaves every ship uncrewed and drifting at its saved velocity.
- Saves from before this rework are not migrated; start a new game or rebuild via the dev "Create two-ship test scene" action (the enemy ship now carries its own console as a valid NPC ship). New dev actions: toggle passive drag, force-split residual blocks on the focused map, and the universe dump now includes per-ship claims and control domains.
- Fixed tactical docking revalidation using a neighboring footprint on even-sized ships: the selected placement root is now encoded back to the vanilla designator's input cell for the final check, while the registered landing position remains unchanged. Orbital and pocket maps now use their owning SpaceObject for one map-to-orbit transform, with the sphere's reverse offset matching its forward movement.
- Unified ship flight state as free flight or docked, separate from faction and derived control state (uncontrolled, manual, autopilot). A single pilot button now changes the active control state. Docking-site selection preserves drift, docked silhouettes follow moving and rotating hosts, and undocking inherits the host's point velocity and angular velocity. Legacy saves migrate the old docking/player flags without adding a new save requirement.
- Tactical docking is now a persistent docking plan instead of a locked cutscene: picking the landing site pauses nothing and consumes nothing (a hollow preview gravship feeds the vanilla picker), and after confirming the site the view returns to your ship with full control — build, plan and issue orders while approaching. A translucent docking silhouette anchored to your ship's pose marks the reserved landing spot on the target (it tracks the target if the target moves); whenever your ship's pose matches the reserved pose inside the configurable alignment tolerance for the configured hold time, docking completes — the ghost turns green the moment you're aligned. Manual piloting and the autopilot dock through the same geometric check; the plan can be cancelled any time (panel button, leaving range, target loss, site invalidated by your own expansion) with nothing consumed until the moment of capture, which happens exactly once at completion so anything you built during the approach travels with the ship. Powered, moving ships are now valid docking targets — the autopilot matches their linear and angular velocity — and the old "only powered-down objects can be landed on" rule is retired (disabling a hostile ship's thrusters is now a tactic, not a requirement).
- The approach autopilot is a tracked-target mode of the generic ShipAutopilot (waypoint and tracked flight, reusable by future ship AI): it recomputes the target pose every tick, including the rotational `omega × offset` velocity of an off-center docking point, while a six-state online MPC replans translation and rotation together at 10 Hz and station-keeps at the reserved pose until cancelled — never auto-"arriving", because completion is the plan's geometric judgment. The autopilot is a special state of "pilot this ship": pilot-key suppression is active while it flies, and pressing any W/A/S/D/Q/E instantly interrupts it and hands you manual control in the same frame (without cancelling the docking plan); resuming autopilot from the panel hands control back. All space-object motion is strictly game-tick driven — pause freezes autopilot, boarding pods and drifting ships alike, and higher game speeds accelerate approaches.
- Added the boarding pod (launcher building on substructure, vacuum barrier research): select the colonists to deploy, fire, and click a target ship's hull — the pod homes in like a missile (no velocity matching needed, marker visible in orbit view), punches through the outer shell, seals the breach with its own landed hull (a door building that never exchanges vacuum) and deploys the crew inside; from then on everything on the target map is vanilla same-map combat. One-way insertion — extract by docking the whole ship or taking the target over. New dev actions support testing: set the enemy ship hostile with a spawned crew, give it drift velocity or spin, and force-complete or cancel the active docking plan.
- Piloting and mode-conversion entries are now faction-gated to player-owned ships (panel buttons, console gizmos and the pilot toggle; developer mode bypasses for testing) — focusing a hostile ship no longer offers to pilot it or launch it.
- New settings: docking hold time (ticks), alignment tolerance (position in cells, angle in degrees — entering tolerance is what turns the ghost green), and docking auto-cancel distance (default 250 cells, below the dormancy range). Saving is blocked only during site selection, arrival replay and pod flights; approach plans are runtime-only, so saving is allowed during flight and loading discards the plan while the ship keeps its saved drift.
- Docking landing-site revalidation now runs the exact vanilla picker chain against the target map with a fresh footprint (rotated to the chosen facing, anchored on the landing root cell) before anything is consumed — expansion during the approach that outgrows or invalidates the site cancels the plan losslessly instead of wedging the transfer; if a recheck ever rejects, the log states the vanilla reason.
- Firing thrusters now play a working loop driven by the allocator's real per-thruster throttles — the same source and 0.25s freshness stamp the flames read, so sound and flames always agree and neither lingers after thrust stops. In the focused map every firing thruster gets its own spatialized loop following the building, with volume per throttle and automatic camera-distance falloff; nearby non-focused ships' shells play one ship-wide loop whose volume scales with total thrust and the distance from the camera to the projected hull (landing approaches included). The clip reuses the vanilla Odyssey gravship engine loop.
- Console ship identities are now written in exactly two situations — console spawn/destroy and the load-time migration for pre-feature saves — and never during takeoffs or landings. The load migration backfills every registered ship's primary console with its object ID and registers the ship's name: saves from before the console-anchor feature carried consoles with no ID at all, so a strategic flight's arrival resolved nothing and stamped a fresh bare ID, leaving the landing site's substructure domain shown as "Independent structure #N" instead of the ship's name. Console-respawn claiming now also covers the ship's own pocket maps (previously guarded off because pocket maps carry no world tile, the very maps a ship spends its life on), and the name registration retries once after the placement batch so the engine's facility link — cleared by the game during flight — can no longer leave a claimed ID nameless. Entering mobile mode now clears the departing ship's ownership entries from the source map wholesale (substructure cannot overlap, so clearing by ID cannot touch anyone else's domain) — host maps no longer keep stale phantom domains behind after a ship lifts off. A strategic launch with several ships docked on the same map now releases the identity of the ship whose grav-engine actually launched instead of whichever docked ship came first.
- Pilot consoles on a shared docked map now resolve their persistent ship identity and owned substructure domain before considering the map's host object. A fixed ship can therefore dock to a wreck, claim and connect part of it, remain fixed, and later launch with the claimed section instead of being mistaken for the host's moving ship.
- Tactical docking (mobile→fixed) no longer triggers any vanilla gravship-landing aftermath: no landing-outcome roll (grav nausea, crash damage, thruster breakdown, or the overheated engine — which also extended the launch cooldown), no scenario landing hooks (the Odyssey start's pursuing-mechanoid warning letter and raids), and never starts or extends the grav-engine cooldown. Stale launch info from an earlier strategic launch can no longer re-roll outcomes on a later docking. Strategic-launch arrivals keep every vanilla outcome, scenario hook and cooldown.
- Strategic-launch departure cleanup is now one sector-driven standard for every source map type. Launching from a wreck-class SpaceObject interior — previously broken, leaving both the sector's world-tile map and the wreck map alive after the launch — now resolves correctly: the owner sector's world-tile map is kept or abandoned by grav-anchor presence regardless of source type (an anchorless world-tile source map is abandoned too, instead of surviving forever), and every other SpaceObject map in the sector, including the departed wreck interior, is put through the beyond-delete-radius path at departure (dormant; snapshot-packed with its map deleted and revisitable later; pawn-blocked maps only sleep) rather than waiting for a distance check that never fires while the ship travels without a player reference. Docked-source launches now also get the sector-abandonment confirmation dialog when their sector map will be abandoned.
- Overlapping non-focused shells now composite as whole objects instead of interleaving building by building: shells draw whole-group in SpaceObject-ID order (later-registered on top) through a camera command buffer, and the focused map's own content always covers every shell. During landing selection and approach the hovering player hull — thruster flames included — temporarily draws above the focused target map, and firing ships' flames no longer float above the focused map's content. Clicking overlapping shells picks the visually topmost one. Added a "shell layering" diagnostic log module (at most one line per second).
- Ship identity now lives permanently on the pilot console: consoles claim a persistent ID the first time they resolve on a space map — from a single monotonic counter shared by ships, POIs and ownership domains, which never reissues an ID even after its object is unregistered — and register their gravship's name for ownership-domain display; every console respawn (gravship landing, mode switch, load) re-stamps the substructure connected to it, absorbing only same-owner or unowned cells. Strategic flights therefore no longer lose the ship's name or ID: landing sites stamp the console's ID, the ownership domain keeps showing the ship's name instead of picking up the landing site's or a freshly spawned derelict's, and independently reassigned structures are never absorbed into the ship's domain or renamed.
- Vanilla strategic gravship launches now work from pocket maps — the ship's own flight pocket in mobile mode, or any fixed-mode docked SpaceObject interior: the destination picker's world view opens at the ship's current sector, and fuel, range, hover labels and range rings all compute from that sector tile (pocket maps carry an invalid tile that previously made every destination unreachable and silently killed the picker UI). Launching from the flight pocket captures the whole connected hull, always destroys the pocket map (stranded pawns abandoned, leftover substructure becomes an unowned wreck), and keeps or abandons the sector's world-tile map by grav-anchor presence with an extra confirmation ahead of the vanilla stranded-pawn warning; launching while docked captures only the grav-engine field under ownership filtering, with departure handled by the sector-driven standard above.
- Fixed↔mobile conversions no longer destroy world-tile maps: the conversion pass leaves world-tile maps to their anchor config and distance lifecycle, deletes blank non-worldtile interiors immediately (their leftover substructure becomes an unowned wreck), and the load-time sweep now only self-heals stale blank pocket maps. Strategic launches follow the sector-driven standard above instead.
- Non-focused object proxies (ships and POIs alike) now share one structural-shell standard instead of two per-kind configs: terrain draws by the vanilla TerrainAt visibility chain (foundations show through open space, floors cover foundations, Space/Void stay invisible), walls, doors, airtight buildings and natural rocks join the shell automatically — small and large thruster bodies now render, falling back to their original graphics when no rule exists — linked graphics pick their atlas piece from the current neighbor connections including vanilla-style corner covers, and things layer by their real def altitude; items and non-airtight furniture stay hidden unless a ShellRenderRuleDef adds them.
- Planet globe: the polar axis now fades out as the sector hex grid fades in; zoomed close, materialized objects' markers are replaced by their true-scale silhouettes, and name/distance labels center directly below the legend (ship triangle, wreck diamond or vanilla icon) instead of the old right-side layout that clipped back across the icon on the narrow panel.
- Thruster flames now render on non-focused ship shells from the allocator's real per-thruster throttles: during the landing approach the shell shows which thrusters actually fire (differential burns and torque couples included), with a 0.25s freshness stamp so flames never linger after thrust stops; in-map flames likewise extend from piloted ships to whatever ship owns the focused frame, so future AI-driven ships will flame in-map too.
- The approach autopilot now performs predictive rigid-body momentum management: online MPC plans ahead across the ship's measured mass, inertia and current thruster authority, then the existing joint allocator remains the sole source of real throttles and achievable force — high-inertia ships start braking before the target instead of oscillating around it. The allocator's own solver is reused for side-effect-free MPC authority previews, while actual commands still follow the exact player-piloting path; docking approaches retain distance-ramped terminal void thrust so arbitrary layouts can close the final alignment error without fake flames.
- Every substructure cell now records its owning SpaceObject ID: grav engines count range and support, vanilla strategic launch, and mobile-mode capture all filter by the ship's own ID (docked ships and wrecks sharing a map keep separate identities), and globe silhouettes are scanned per (map, owner) so a ship docked onto a wreck draws its own shape while the wreck draws its own.
- Substructure build rules follow ownership: a new cell inherits the single distinct valid owner among its four neighbors (and absorbs the entire connected unowned region, so multi-row builds end up consistent regardless of build order), stays unowned when built in isolation (no ID until connected or claimed via a pilot-console bootstrap, which claims the whole domain), and building onto multiple different owners is rejected with a placement preview that colors neighboring owners.
- Landing site rules reworked: ships may now land in pure open space (no solid-surface contact required) but the footprint may not overlap or touch foreign-owned substructure — cross-structure connections go through docking ports; same-owner leftovers are still allowed, and the initial landing cell spirals outward to the nearest valid spot when the hover projection lands on the wreck deck.
- Added the docking port (door building, vacuum barrier research, rotatable): entering fixed mode auto-extends a sealed docking channel (walkable terrain plus constructed roof, natively airtight through vanilla room mechanics) in its facing direction up to the first structure, with manual extend/retract gizmos; destroying, deconstructing or launching with the port retracts the channel and restores open space. The channel is not substructure: it never joins engine domains, capture, or silhouettes.
- Added the "Reassign substructure" order (Orders category, next to deconstruct/mine): pick a target owner from every owner present on the map plus a new-independent-ID option, then paint cells zone-style; while selected the map shows every substructure colored by owner with floating name labels, and dragged cells highlight in the target owner's color.
- Fixed docked-ship globe silhouettes drawing 180° rotated (facing east while actually facing west): silhouette rotation is now paired with the texture's source frame via a single ShipSilhouette entry point (pocket-map textures rotate by the ship pose angle, host-map textures by the host frame angle); the world-view marker's lazy rebuild after loading a docked save had the same class of bug and is fixed too.
- Wreck POI templates now generate asymmetric hulls (random orientation, side protrusion, corner bite, ragged erosion, and a detached fragment) — silhouette shape/orientation bugs can no longer hide behind rectangular symmetry; existing saved wrecks keep their old shape.
- Saves with stale substructure ownership (from pre-fix builds where pocket placements got orphan IDs) self-heal on load: a pocket map whose ship owns no cells is reclaimed wholesale, and a dev action resets a map's ownership to its default owner for manual repair.
- Landing selection now keeps the player hull visible end to end without an extra overlay: the site-selection snapshot removes nothing from the pocket map, so the standard non-focused shell keeps drawing the hovering ship on the focused target map during selection and approach, and the planet-globe silhouette (yellow focus tint) no longer disappears mid-docking (a docking skip in the globe ship loop and a stale capture-era skip in the shell renderer were removed).
- Placement anchors are now explicit at the API level: SpaceTransform gained a placement-anchor section (adjusted cell ↔ placement root, placement root → ship-frame origin, engine root → gravship bounds center, cell-set bounds center) and every call site — landing pose reverse-mapping, initial landing cell, takeoff structure center, bootstrap, and the adjacency relaxation patch (which drops its reflection of the designator's private anchor math) — goes through it; numerically equivalent, no behavior change.
- Restructured tactical landing to keep the ship fully in its pocket map end to end: site selection now only freezes a non-removing gravship manifest snapshot (zones, floor designations and storage groups move into the manifest), so the hull, substructure and globe silhouette no longer have a limbo state during selection or approach; the autopilot arrival signal triggers the vanilla removal pass (strip the pocket map's substructure) followed by the vanilla placement pipeline at the chosen site.
- Docking target selection now breaks projection overlaps by nearest visible hull (convex-hull distance between the player's hull cells and each target's renderable hull, matching what you actually see), instead of render order — fixing landings onto a small-hulled object whose larger map happened to cover another object's visible shell.
- Fixed landing approach rotation and extracted a reusable orbit-layer autopilot: the target pose for the approach is now reverse-mapped from the chosen landing cell/rotation (host frame plus landing-local offset, angle = host frame angle plus final placement facing — read before the pocket-layout restore), so the approaching hull now turns to the commanded orientation on any host including angled wreck interiors; takeoff and bootstrap headings fold in the host frame angle symmetrically so dock/undock attitudes stay continuous. The flight itself moved into a generic ShipAutopilot (fly a ship to an orbit position and angle with arrival/failure signals plus an awaitable wrapper) that future ship AI can reuse.
- Reworked the tactical landing sequence into an approach autopilot: after confirming the landing site the ship stays in its flight pocket map, the target map keeps rendering normally (buildings and pawns stay visible), and the player hull is drawn by the standard non-focused shell renderer while the reusable guidance controller flies the ship onto the chosen pose; on arrival the vanilla landing pipeline is replayed with a zero-length animation to perform the real transfer, replacing the old custom interpolated animation that hid the map behind camera masks.
- Fixed globe silhouettes rendering mirrored (a right-side extension now shows on the right): all silhouette textures are built through a single ShipSilhouette utility in canonical orientation, and world-view ship markers now use the engine's full connected substructure domain, matching mobile-mode capture scope.
- Landing markers can no longer spawn out of bounds: initial landing cells are clamped to the ship footprint and gated by the vanilla designator check (falling back to manual placement when the map is smaller than the ship); blank canvas anchors are no longer tactical docking targets (canvases with content remain landable).
- Strategic gravship arrivals on Delta-V maps now freeze the incoming hull foundation for the landing cutscene (previously only tactical landings did), keeping adjacent wrecks out of the landing animation and hull mask.
- Fixed gravship transition presentation and capture semantics: vanilla strategic launch/landing cutscenes no longer inherit Delta-V's extended universe zoom; Delta-V tactical landing shows the live target map instead of a distorted terrain curtain and freezes only the incoming hull for the animation so nearby wrecks do not contaminate it. Once landed, all connected substructure is unified again and mobile-mode takeoff carries the pilot console's entire connected structure, while vanilla strategic gravship launch remains limited to the grav-engine field.
- The sector map is now the panel planet globe with continuous zoom (slider under the globe or mouse wheel over it): zooming in progressively reveals the sector hex grid (true world-tile vertices), object icons (vanilla expanding icons with faction colors; derelicts as diamonds) and all ships including hostile ones, then name and distance labels; LOD thresholds track the sector-scale setting.
- Zoomed close, materialized objects (ships and derelicts) switch to true-scale hull silhouettes rotated to their pose (substructure scan, world-view-snapshot semantics), and the deepest zoom spans six times the focused ship's longest edge for docking approach guidance; all globe line work is clipped to the globe rect.
- Pocket-map exteriors (ships and derelicts) are now filled with vanilla Space vacuum instead of the opaque Void terrain: decks can be extended outward through the vanilla gravship foundation mechanics, ships can land on open ground next to wrecks, and EVA-style exterior work has a terrain basis; existing saves keep their old Void maps.
- Entering a previously dormant or POI-interior map now force-regenerates all map meshes on entry (dirty sections accumulated during frozen MapUpdate skips rendered blank until a build event); added a focused-map diagnostics dev action, and wreck thrusters now spawn with a valid rotation (their texture was broken).
- Globe icons and ships are hoverable (name, distance, not-in-range note) and clickable to focus their map; clicking an object that has not materialized yet shows a "not in visual range" hint.
- Player ship silhouettes in the world view are now clickable to focus the ship (manual screen picking with vanilla horizon culling; they deliberately stay out of the vanilla world selection panel).
- Implemented the star sector model: world tiles are now coarse addresses and bookkeeping units only, while all tactical geometry (collision, landing site selection) happens in continuous orbit coordinates; sector scale (cells per world tile) is a setting (default 20000, 100-100000) that rescales all object poses around their sector centers on change, keeps velocities untouched, is blocked during docking/launch, and re-basess poses after load when changed from the main menu.
- Reworked tactical docking: the ship can enter fixed mode only when its center lies inside the internal-map projection of an unpowered SpaceObject (vanilla sites, blank canvases, stations, wrecks, and disabled ships; a ship with no working thrusters or no intact pilot consoles counts as unpowered and becomes landable); the initial landing marker is placed at the mapped hover position and the vanilla selector still allows adjustment; deep-space docking is refused instead of generating a blank canvas.
- Landing now also works into pocket-map interiors (wrecks, stations, disabled ships), and takeoff computes the new pose as host pose plus the internal takeoff point for any host orientation; vanilla gravship launches from pocket maps use the sector tile of the ship's pose as their strategic source address and release the Delta-V identity until re-bootstrap.
- Added per-sector content bookkeeping: entering a sector for the first time (mobile-mode crossing, gravship arrival, or game start) spawns Delta-V derelicts under configurable rules (default minimum 1 per sector, values still provisional); the "already generated" flag persists across saves while object lists are rebuilt on demand.
- Expanded universe governance: blank-canvas anchors (vanilla Space sites and DeltaV_SpaceHostParent) and host maps occupied by docked ships are now governed SpaceObjects, so every enterable map belongs to one; empty blank canvases are still removed on departure and on load.
- Added SpaceObject faction fields (persisted) and a thruster power-source abstraction: any building with a gravship thruster comp now counts as a thruster, thruster availability is read dynamically, and inoperable thrusters no longer produce thrust.
- Replaced the mobile-mode HUD with a single auto-sized left status panel (no scrolling) containing the mode buttons (piloting toggle, enter fixed/mobile mode; single-console maps can bootstrap directly, multi-ship maps direct you to a specific console), a disabled sector-map placeholder, and a wireframe planet globe (north-up with lat/long grid and back-side culling, cursor-style ship markers with heading and velocity arrows, pole roll fallback).
- Removed the landing-map-size setting and its dev actions; added sector force-generate and expanded universe-state dump dev actions.
- Fixed an InvalidOperationException on first entry into each new sector (sector bookkeeping registered derelicts while lazily enumerating the universe object list), gated sector bookkeeping to space layers only so RimExodus surface arrivals are never touched, and fixed MakeThing errors when stamping wreck loot.

- Fixed mapless orbital POIs (asteroids, settlements, and other orbital map parents) being silently dropped from universe governance: the ship-occupancy check compared a null parent map against a null docked map and rejected every POI that had never generated a map, so proximity generation never triggered.
- Fixed a save-load warning about unconsumed hostMap load IDs on saves from the planar-coordinate era.
- Added player-ship silhouette markers to the orbit-layer world view; each marker follows the ship's continuous spherical position and orientation while reusing a one-time hull snapshot captured on entry to mobile mode.
- Replaced the planar universe frame and dynamic-anchor bookkeeping with absolute spherical coordinates on the orbit layer; removed floating-origin shifts and tile re-anchoring entirely.
- Replaced the player-flow host map with a dedicated tile-less universe host pocket map: all Delta-V pocket maps (ships and POIs) no longer occupy orbit tiles, are hidden from the world view, and the host never ticks or takes focus.
- Decoupled takeoff and landing from the host: landing targets the real orbit map at the ship's sphere position (reused when one exists, generated otherwise), and any real orbit map—including vanilla gravship maps created on POI tiles—can now bootstrap directly into mobile mode.
- Old saves migrate automatically: planar positions convert to sphere directions and stale pocket-map tiles are cleared on load.
- Empty landing maps are now removed: after a ship departs, a space map left with no pawns, buildings, items, or docked ships is deleted outright (also swept on load) instead of lingering as empty orbit maps.
- Fixed per-tick season/ritual errors in mobile mode: tile-less ship pocket maps no longer count as the player home map (matching vanilla gravship transit); the real landing map becomes home once the ship touches down.
- Fixed tile-less mobile ship maps reaching vanilla celestial, weather, and local-date calculations with tile ID -1; their environment is now sampled dynamically from the ship's spherical position without occupying a world tile.
- Added stage 3 space POI lifecycle, dynamic universe anchoring, extended zoom, and background reference-frame rotation.
- Extended the vanilla Odyssey pilot console with Delta-V piloting and primary-console controls.
- Added fixed-orbit docking through the vanilla Gravship landing selector and placement pipeline, with a fixed-zoom pose-transition landing animation.
