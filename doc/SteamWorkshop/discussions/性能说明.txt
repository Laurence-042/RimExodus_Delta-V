[h1]DeltaV的主要性能消耗[/h1]

[h1]Delta-V's main performance costs[/h1]

主要性能消耗基本在战斗层面上，日常消耗其实本身不多，同时多地图的机制可能会放大一些mod的问题。

Most of the performance cost comes from combat. Day-to-day overhead is fairly low, although the multiple-map system can amplify problems in some other mods.

[b]战斗[/b]

[b]Combat[/b]

成百上千的飞行弹丸状态更新、碰撞检测本身就是非常严重的性能消耗。

Updating the state of hundreds or thousands of flying projectiles and checking their collisions is already very expensive.

除此之外，飞船ai进行战斗决策时也需要评估自己和对方的防护薄弱点以及武器火力、射程配比，以此决定机动目标。

Ship AI also needs to assess its own and its opponent's weak points, firepower, and weapon-range distribution when deciding where to maneuver.

而战斗很容易触发高频的基架损毁，虽然用了比较高效的算法来保证这种情况的性能，但毕竟不是免费的，而且发生大面积断裂时仍然会产生较大的消耗

Combat can destroy substructure at high frequency. A relatively efficient algorithm keeps this work manageable, but it is not free, and large breakups can still be expensive.

这些都导致战斗状态甚至很难维持3倍速

Together, these costs can make it difficult to maintain even triple speed during combat.

[b]日常[/b]

[b]Day-to-day play[/b]

对于非战斗状态，性能主要消耗点在于自动驾驶导航的MPC求解、推进器控制之类的上面

Outside combat, the main costs come from MPC solving for autopilot navigation, thruster control, and related work.

总体来说，除了这些之外，没有添加什么额外的常驻性能消耗点

Beyond that, Delta-V adds little continuous overhead.

当然，严格来说武器的绘制、飞船的绘制、货柜温控插件的耗电之类的也是常驻性能消耗，但是基本都可以忽略不计或者不怎么影响tps

Strictly speaking, rendering weapons and ships, powering cargo-container temperature-control modules, and similar work also runs continuously, but these costs can usually be ignored or have little effect on TPS.

[b]机制[/b]

[b]Mechanics[/b]

因为DeltaV是使用单独的口袋地图维护每一组连续结构的，所以自然会出现大量的口袋地图。有些mod可能会在每张地图生成时进行大量与地图面积、内容复杂度无关的复杂初始化，DeltaV的大量地图可能会导致其问题被显著放大。

Delta-V maintains each group of connected structures on a separate pocket map, which naturally creates a large number of pocket maps. Some mods may perform expensive initialization on every map regardless of its area or content. Delta-V's large number of maps can magnify those problems considerably.

[h1]卡的时候可以先做什么[/h1]

[h1]What to try when the game is slow[/h1]

交火时建议 1 倍速，当前射速、冷却之类的都是按1倍速调的，更高倍速很难反应过来。

I recommend using 1× speed during combat. Current fire rates, cooldowns, and similar values are tuned for 1×, and higher speeds leave very little time to react.

初次接近大型站点时，生成内部地图可能会有段时间较为卡顿（虽然已经尽量用分帧生成的机制避免冻结了……）。

The first approach to a large station may stutter while its internal map is generated, although generation is already spread across frames as much as possible to avoid a complete freeze.

如果您发现确实十分卡顿，可以打开「模组设置 → Delta-V → 日志 → 性能分析」，保持同一视角和倍速，记录至少三个五秒窗口。然后发送 Player.log 里卡顿前后的 [DeltaV:Performance] 行。

If you encounter serious stuttering, enable “Mod settings → Delta-V → Logs → Performance analysis”. Keep the same camera view and speed, and record at least three five-second windows. Then send the [DeltaV:Performance] lines from Player.log immediately before and after the stutter.
