[h1]整体计划[/h1]

[h1]Overall plan[/h1]

当前状态为 alpha测试，比起一个完善mod更接近概念展示demo，以展示功能为主，尚不能提供完整的游玩循环

The mod is currently in alpha testing. This is closer to a proof-of-concept demo than a complete mod. Its current purpose is to demonstrate features, and it does not yet provide a complete gameplay loop.

计划在接下来的1-2个月内结合玩家反馈，在 0.x.x 的版本中逐渐完善必要的功能以及补充内容，当前足够提供完整的游玩循环时会进入 1.0.0 的正式版本

Over the next 1-2 months, I plan to use player feedback to refine the essential features and add more content through the 0.x.x releases. The mod will move to the full 1.0.0 release once it can provide a complete gameplay loop.

[h1]已知问题[/h1]

[h1]Known issues[/h1]

目前玩家未停靠在worldtile地图的飞船不会被敌人主动攻击，除非飞到敌人附近。但还不确定要不要加随机遇敌

At present, enemies do not proactively attack player ships that are not docked on world-tile maps unless those ships fly near the enemy. I still have not decided whether to add random encounters.

无人的地图有点难在大地图上找到（宇宙地图可以一定程度上改善这一点，但是我觉得在原版大地图上也该易用）

Maps without people are somewhat difficult to find on the world map. The sector map helps to some extent, but I think they should also be easy to locate on the vanilla world map.

当前常规飞行不会消耗化合燃料，我有在考虑添加相关消耗机制，但是总不能让玩家一不小心变太空垃圾还无法挽回，所以需要先开发救援机制，但具体咋做还没想好，想不好的话我没准就不做化合燃料消耗了

Regular flight currently consumes no chemfuel. I am considering adding fuel consumption, but I cannot simply let players accidentally become irrecoverable space debris. A rescue system needs to be developed first. I still have no concrete design for it, and if I cannot work one out, I may simply leave regular flight without chemfuel consumption.

非聚焦视图上只有墙体类型建筑才会被绘制，这导致大部分建筑都不会在非聚焦地图上绘制（比如商会的那个轨道炮），准备近期看看能不能渲染的同时避免fps下降

Only wall-type buildings are rendered in the non-focused view. As a result, most buildings do not appear on non-focused maps, including the Trade Guild's orbital cannon. I plan to look soon at whether those buildings can be rendered without reducing FPS.

跳帮艇被击毁后没有人员死亡提示，准备近期修复

There is no death notification when a boarding shuttle is destroyed. I plan to fix this soon.

[h1]当前高风险功能[/h1]

[h1]Current high-risk features[/h1]

Prefab、任务系统、地图归档与恢复尚未得到充分验证

Prefabs, the mission system, and map archiving and restoration have not been tested thoroughly.

程序化舰船生成目前表现无法实用化

Procedural ship generation is not currently practical.

整体内容过于贫瘠，质量可接受的舰船只有手工制作的几个

The mod is far too short on content, and only a few hand-built ships meet an acceptable quality level.

[h1]目前不稳定的功能[/h1]

[h1]Features that are currently unstable[/h1]

自动驾驶的存档结构尚未完全敲定，需要等到无人机机制完成后才能彻底敲定，所以目前自动驾驶状态没进存档，存档读档后自动驾驶状态会丢失。

The autopilot save-data structure cannot be finalized until the drone system is complete. Autopilot state is therefore not saved at present and is lost when a save is loaded.

自动战斗AI目前的自定义能力尚没有得到充分测试，不排除之后大改成其他编辑模式的可能，所以目前不建议在AI自定义上投入太多精力。

The customization available for automated combat AI has not been tested thoroughly. Its editor may still be redesigned completely, so I do not recommend investing too much time in AI customization yet.
